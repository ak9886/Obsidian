"For they wanted more than they could keep,
they reached their hands into the caves of Hertz,
Burned etchings onto its walls -- in the"purest language". They Commanded nature to follow.

Nature followed,
Their boon was given,
Now they roam across dimensions,
how they wanted.
But without life, and without home.

They forgot causality,
The first rule of their trade,
Exchanges are of equal and opposite value"
