#AppSec
# Key Components & Tools
- Indicators of Compromise (IoCs): IP Addresses, domain names, file hashes, URLs, registry keys, email addresses
- Tactics, Techniques, and Procedures (TTP): How adversaries operate (e.g. MITRE ATT&CK Framework)
- Security Information and Event Management (SIEM): Correlates security events, often ingests threat feeds.
- Endpoint Detection and Response (EDR): Monitors and collects data from endpoints, can leverage TI for Detection
- Vulnerability Management Solutions: Prioritize patching based on active threats.
# Challenges in Threat Intelligence
- Volume and Velocity of Data: Overwhelming amount of raw data.
- Noise vs. Signal: Distinguish relevant threats from false positives.
- Integration: Integrating TI into existing security tools and workflows
- Skill Shortage: Lack of skilled analysts to interpret and act on intelligence.
- Timeliness: Intelligence can quickly become outdated.
- Actionability: Ensuring intelligence leads to concrete defensive actions.
# Implementing a Threat Intelligence Program
- Define clear goals and objectives
- Identify critical assets and potential attack surfaces.
- Establish a dedicated threat intelligence team or integrate into existing teams.
