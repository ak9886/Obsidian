# Water Pollution
- Urban cities - 65% waste water (Water is not treated) -> When the water is not treated, it comes back as our drinking water, leading to diseases like Cancer
- 71% of the Earth's surface is water
	- 97% salt water
	- 3% fresh water
		- 87% ice and glaciers, underground, air
		- 13% surface water (0,4% total water)
- Ideal water supply
	- Quality
	- Quantity
- Water quality parameter
	- Physical
		- Suspended solid (SS), color, smell, taste, temp
	- Chemical
		- Dissolved substances, alkalinity, hardness, fluoride, heavy metal, organic compound, nutrient (nitrogen & phosphorus), pH, biochemical oxygen demand, chemical oxygen demand
- 70% of water in India has been deemed unfit for consumption
## Sources of Water Pollution

- Residential Buildings
- Sewage treatment Facilities
- Urban Run-off
- Livestock
- Agriculture
- Landfills
- Atmospheric Deposition
