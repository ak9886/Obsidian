# Applications of Nanotechnology - Food Packaging and Food Safety
## Introduction
### Packaging
Scientific Method of enclosing food materials/goods in a container and it ensures the delivery of goods to the ultimate consumer in the best condition intended for their use. 
	~={red}(_Robertson, G.L., 2005_)=~

### Functions of Packaging
- Containment
- Protection
- Convenient
- Communication

- Food being a putrescible commodity is highly susceptible to contamination and spoilage
- The quality of the food can be compromised at any stage of the food delivery chain.
