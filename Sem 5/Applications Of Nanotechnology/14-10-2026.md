## Actuators
Hardware devices that convert a controller command signal into a chance in a physical parameter.
- The change is usually mechanical (Position or Velocity).
- An actuator is also a transducer because it changes one type of physical quantity into some alternative form.
- An actuator is usually activated by a low-level command signal, so an amplifier may be required to provide the sufficient power to drive the actuator.
This is how Hard Disks store data, by using actuators to flip the charges as N or S, to represent 1 and 0.
