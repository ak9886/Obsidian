# Nanotechnology in Agriculture
1700s - Industrial Revolution

1960s - Green Revolution

> _Nanotechnology deals with the matter at nanoscale (1-100nm) is commonly referred as a generic technology that offers better built, safer, long-lasting, cost-effective and smart products that will find wide application household, communication, medicine, agriculture and food._

It is an emerging and fast growing field of science which is being exploited at a wide spectrum

Precision Farming is a better option to reduce production costs and to maximize output, i.e., agricultural production. The process of maximizing crop yields and minimixing the usage of pesticides, fertilizers and herbicides through effecient monitoring procedures is referred to as precision farming
