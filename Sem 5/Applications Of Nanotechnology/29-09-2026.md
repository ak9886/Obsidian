## What is Quantum Mechanics?
QM is the theory of the behavior of very small objects (i.e. molecules, atoms, nuclei, elementary particles, quantum fields, etc.)
### Why QM?
One of the essential differences between classical and quantum mechanics is that physical variables that can take on continuous values in classic mechanics (e.g., energy, angular momentum).
## Two types of Approaches
- Top-Down: Take a Macro-material, and whittle it down to a nano-material
- Bottom-Up: Use individual nano-particles to construct a bigger nano-material
