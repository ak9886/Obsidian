# The sponsor
An information security risk assessment is usually seen as a project or an initiative that is part of the overall enterprise information security program or enterprise risk management process.
Typically, the decision of who the project sponsor should be is not a difficult one. As mentioned previously, it should be the person or group, who has the highest level of accountability for security risk within the organization.
Typically this could be the:
- VP of Information Technology
- Chief Information Officer
- Chief Technical Officer
- Chief Information Security Officer
- An Executive board or committee (e.g.)
