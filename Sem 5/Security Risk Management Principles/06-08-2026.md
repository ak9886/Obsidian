# Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA)
- Is a federal law that is designed to protect the privacy and security of patient health information
### As a result:
- The patient's control of and access to their health care info has increased
- Protections for individually identifiable health information from threats of loss or unauthorized disclosure have increased substantially
# HIPAA Workflow
Understand HIPAA -> baseline the organization -> Plan Compliance Strategies -> Remediate the Organization -> Validate Compliance -> Maintain Complicance
