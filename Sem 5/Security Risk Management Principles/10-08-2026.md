### Introduction to Data Collection
#### Data Sources
Data can be collected from a wide range of sources, including internal and external databases, security incidents and breaches, threat intelligence feeds, employee reports, security assessments, regulatory compliance reports, and more. The quality and diversity of data sources influence the accuracy of the risk.
