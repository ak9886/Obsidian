## Info Risk Management Activities

The four stages of Malware:
- Accepting the risk
- Avoiding the risk
- Transferring the risk
- Mitigating the risk
Equifax Data Breach - 2017

NIST FISMA COMPLIANCE:
- Categorize the info to be protected
- Select minimum baseline controls
- Refine controls using a risk assessment procedure
- Document the controls in the system security plan
- Implement security controls in appropriate info systems
- Assess the effectiveness of the controls
