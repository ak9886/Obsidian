# 1) Introduction to Risk
## What is Risk?
- Risk is the potential for **Loss or harm due to likelihood of a threat** exploiting vulnerability.
- Risk can be defined as a situation that exposes an object to harm.
- Risk can be defined as the measurement of uncertainity.
NIST Definition
- A measure of the extent to which an entity is threatened by a potentional circumstance or event, and typically a function of:
	- The adverse impacts that would arise if the circumstance or event occurs, and
	- The likelihood of occurence
## 1.a) Components of Risk
### The genetic components of risk are:
- Event
- Probability
- Asset
- Outcome
### The components of information security risk are: 
- Threat
- Vulnerabilities
- Asset

# 3) Information Security Risk Management Overview
- Information Security Risk Management, or ISRM, is the process of managing risks associated with the use of infosec.
- It involves identifying, assessing and treating risks to the confidentiality, integrity and availability of an organization's assets.
- The End goal of this process is to treat risks in accordance with an organization's overall risk tolerance.
- Business shouldn't expect to eliminate all risks; rather, they should seek to identify and achieve acceptable risks for their organization
- The security program should be filling a governance and oversight role to help identify the risks that have the greatest chance of harming the organization with the most severe impact.
## Four Stages ISRM
- The four stages of information security risk management.
1) Identification
	- The first step of ISRM is to identify all the organization's assets, vulnerabilities, threats and controls.
2) Assessment
	- Once you have identified all assets, vulnerabilities, threats and controls, you can access the risks.
3) Treatment
	- Once you have assessed the risks, you can develop and implement risk treatment plans
4) Monitoring & Reporting
	- Information security risk management is a cont. process. you must monitor risks and update treatment plans regularly because new assets, vuln., threats and controls are constantly emerging.
