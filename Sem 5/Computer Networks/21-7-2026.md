# Classification by Network Geography
- Networks are frequently classified according to the geographical boundaries spanned by the network itself.
- LAN, WAN, PAN and MAN are the basic types of classification, of which LAN and WAN are frequently used.

## 1. LAN
- A Local Area Network (LAN) is usually privately owned and connects some hosts in a single office, building, or campus. Depending on the needs of an organization, a LAN can be as simple as 2 Pcs and a printer in someone's home office, or it can extend throughout a company and include audio and video devices. LANs are inexpensive to install and also provide higher speeds. 
- LAN also provides Higher Security
