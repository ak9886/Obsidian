# Approaches of Packet Switching
- The message is divided into smaller pieces called packets and sent individually.
- it is a combination of circuit and message switching
- Packets are given a unique number to identify their order at the receiving end.
- Every packet contains some information in its headers such as source address, destination address and sequence numbers
- All the packets are re-assembled at the receiving end in the correct order
- If a packet is missing or corrupted, then the message will be sent to resend the message.
- If the correct order of the packets is reached, an acknowledgement message will be sent to the sender
There are two approaches to Packet Switching:
### Datagram Packet Switching
- It is a packet switching technique in which the packet is known as a datagram, which is considered as an independent entity. Each packets contains the information about the destination and switch uses this information to forward the packet to the correct destination.
- The packets are re-assembled at the receiving end in the correct order.
- In datagram packet switching technique, the path is not fixed.
- Intermediate nodes take the routing decisions to forward the packets.
- Datagram Packet Switching is also known as connectionless switching.
