# Substitution Techniques
- A sub technique is one in which the letters of plaintext are replaced by other letters or by numbers or symbols.
- If plaintext is viewed as a sequence of bits, then substitution involves replacing plaintext bit patterns with ciphertext bit patterns
- Techniques
	- Caesar Cipher
	- Monoaplphabetic
