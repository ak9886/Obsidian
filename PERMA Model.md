#SE 
The PERMA model is an abbreviation of:
- **P – Positive Emotion**  
    Experiencing joy, gratitude, hope, contentment, and other uplifting emotions.
- **E – Engagement**  
    Being deeply involved or absorbed in activities (often described as a “flow” state).
- **R – Relationships**  
    Having meaningful, supportive connections with others.
- **M – Meaning**  
    Belonging to and serving something bigger than oneself.
- **A – Accomplishment**  
    Pursuing and achieving goals, mastery, or success.
