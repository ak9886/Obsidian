# Mathematical Induction
## 1+2+3+.... +n+(n+1) = ((n(n+1))/2)

## 1+3+5+...+(2n-3)+(2n-1) = n<sup>2</sup>

## 2+4+6+...+(2n-2)+(2n) = n(n+1)

## 1<sup>3</sup>+2<sup>3</sup>+3<sup>3</sup>+..+ (n-1)<sup>3</sup>+n<sup>3</sup> = ((n<sup>2</sup>(n+1)<sup>2</sup>)/4)

## 1<sup>2</sup>+2<sup>2</sup>+3<sup>2</sup>+...+(n-1)<sup>2</sup>+n<sup>2</sup> = ((n(n+1)(2n+1))/6)
