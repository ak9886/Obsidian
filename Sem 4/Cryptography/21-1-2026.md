# OSI Security Architecture
OSI - Open System Interconnect 
## What is Info Sec?
It is the protection of Information with three different ways
Comprised of:
- Computer Sec
- Network Sec
- Internet Sec
### Computer Security
The protection afforded to an automated information system in order to attain the applicable objectives of preserving the integrity, availability and confidentiality of information system resources (including hardware, software, firmware, information/data and telecommunications)
One cannot exist without the other two
#### Confidentiality:
- Data Confidentiality: Assures that private or confidential info is not makde available or disclosed to unathourized individuals
- Privacy: Assures that individuals control or influence what info related to them may be collected and stored by whom and to whom that info is discloed
#### Integrity:
- Data Integrity: Assures that information and programs are changed only in a specific and authorized manner
- System Integrity: Assures that systems perform its intended function in an unimpaired manner, free from deliberate or inadvertent unauthorized manipulatioin
