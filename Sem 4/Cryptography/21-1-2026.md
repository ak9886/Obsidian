# OSI Security Architecture
OSI - Open System Interconnect 
## What is Info Sec?
It is the protection of Information with three different ways
Comprised of:
- Computer Sec
- Network Sec
- Internet Sec
### Computer Security
The protection afforded to an automated information system in order to attain the applicable objectives of preserving the integrity, availability and confidentiality of information system resources (including hardware, software, firmware, information/data and telecommunications)
One cannot exist without the other two
#### Confidentiality:
- <span style='1.Sem'>**Data Confidentiality**: Assures that private or confidential info is not make available or disclosed to unauthorized individuals
- **Privacy**: Assures that individuals control or influence what info related to them may be collected and stored by whom and to whom that info is disclosed
#### Integrity:
- **Data Integrity**: Assures that information and programs are changed only in a specific and authorized manner
- **System Integrity**: Assures that systems perform its intended function in an unimpaired manner, free from deliberate or inadvertent unauthorized manipulation of the system.
#### Availability:
- Assures that systems work promptly and service is not denied to authorized users.


The OSI Security Arch focuses on:
- Security Attacks
- Security Mechanisms
- Security 


#### Security Attack
- Any Action that compromises the security of information owned by an organization.
- Classification: 
	- A Passive attack attempts to learn or make use of information from the system but does not affect system resources
	- An Active attack does the oppposite
##### Passive Attacks
- Eavesdropping
- Monitoring of transmission
- Two types:
	- Release of message contents
	- Traffic Analysis
##### Active Attacks
- Release of message contents
	- A telephone conversation, an electronic mail message, and a transferred file may be monitored
- Four types of attacks
	- Masquerade
	- Replay
	- Modification of messages
	- Denial of Service
