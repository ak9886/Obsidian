# The Division Algorithm
- Given any positive integer n and any  non-negative integer a, if we divide a by n, we get an integer quotient q and an integer remainder r that obeys the following relationship:
	- `a=qn+r,    where 0<=r<n; q=[a/n]`
## Quite Important
# Euclidean Algorithm
- Euclid is used to easily find the greatest common division of two integers.
- The greatest common divisor of a and b notation gcd(a,b)
- The greatest common divisor of a and b is the largest integer that divides both a and b.
- We also define gcd(0,0)=0
- The positive integer c is said to be the greatest common divisor
