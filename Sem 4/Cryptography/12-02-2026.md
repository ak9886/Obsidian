# Data Encryption Standard (DES)
- Symmetric Key Algorithm for the encryption of electronic data.
- The Data Encryption Standard was published by the National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST)
- For DES, data is encrypted in 64-bit blocks using a 64-bit key.
- The algorithm transforms 64-bit input in a series of steps into a 64-bit output
- ## Input
	- Plaintext (64-bit in length)
	- Key (64-bit length)
- ## 3 Phases
	- First Phase: Initial Permutation, Permuted Choice 1
