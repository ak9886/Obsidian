# Entity Relationship Diagram (ER Diagram)
- An ER diagram (Entity-Relationship Diagram) is a graphical representation of a database structure that shows entities, their attributes, and the relationships b/w the entities.
- It helps us **Design a database** by visually modelling **real-world objects** (like Student, City, Order) and how they are connected.
## Purpose:
- To analyze requirements
- To design the database before implementation
- To reduce redundancy and errors
