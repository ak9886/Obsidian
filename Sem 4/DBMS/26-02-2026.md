#DBMS 

| Operator          | Symbol | Meaning                                                         |
| ----------------- | ------ | --------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Selection         | σ      | Selects tuples (rows) that satisfy a given condition            |
| Projection        | π      | Selects specific attributes (columns) from a relation           |
| Union             | ∪      | Combines tuples from two relations (must be union-compatible)   |
| Difference        | −      | Returns tuples present in one relation but not in another       |
| Cartesian Product | ×      | Combines all tuples of one relation with all tuples of another  |
| Join              | ⨝      | Combines related tuples from two relations based on a condition |
| Rename            | ρ      | Renames a relation or its attributes                            |
