# Semantic Networks
- Used as a knowledge representation technique; in the form of a graph.
	- Nodes -> Object
	- Links -> Relationship
## Examples
- Dog - <u>is an</u> - Animal
- Dog - <u>has</u> - tail
- Wheel <u>is a part of a</u> - car

## Types of Relation
1) Classification
2) Possession
3) Component
